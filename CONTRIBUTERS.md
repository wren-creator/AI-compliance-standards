# Contributors Guide

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the **AI-Compliance-Standards** project.  
This repository is an evolving workspace dedicated to building future global frameworks for AI governance, compliance, and risk management.

We welcome contributions of all types; from minor edits to major standards development.

---

## Before Contributing

Please review the following existing documents to ensure your contributions align with the project’s standards:

- **[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)**: Expected community behavior.
- **[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)**: Responsible security reporting.
- **[README.md](./README.md)**: Repository overview and directory structure, including the AI‑BOM module.
- **[AI-BOM Subdirectory](./AI-BOM)**: Contains its own structure and documentation.

---

## Ways to Contribute

### **Documentation**
- Fix grammar, formatting, and clarity.
- Improve structure or add missing context.
- Add diagrams, tables, or cross‑references.

### **Standards Development**
- Propose or revise sections in:
  - *AI Compliance Master Controls-BOM (Bill of Materials)*
  - *Evidence Checklists & Procedures*

### **Technical Contributions**
- Provide example implementations.
- Add templates or automation steps for compliance workflows.

### **Review and Feedback**
- Comment on existing drafts.
- Validate terminology consistency.
- Participate in issue discussions.

---

## Contribution Workflow

1. **Fork the repository**
2. **Create a descriptive branch**, e.g.:
   - `docs/update-ai-bom-structure`
   - `feature/add-governance-controls`
   - `fix/typo-in-procedures`

3. **Submit a Pull Request**
   Include:
   - A short summary
   - Why the change is useful
   - Related issue numbers (if any)
