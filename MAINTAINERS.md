## Maintainer Responsibilities

### Repository Stewardship
- Guide project vision and maintain documentation clarity.
- Approve or request changes on Pull Requests.
- Ensure consistency across standards, templates, and checklists.

### Community & Contribution Management
- Apply the **[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)* to maintain a respectful space.
- Provide constructive feedback on contributions.
- Manage contributor acknowledgments in **[CONTRIBUTORS.md](./CONTRIBTERS.md)*.

### Standards Governance
- Review updates to:
  - *AI-BOM*
  - *Master Controls Catalog*
  - *Procedures & Evidence Checklists*
- Maintain version alignment and terminology consistency.

### Security Coordination
- Follow practices defined in **[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)**.
- Respond to disclosures through the documented process.

---

## Pull Request Review Guidelines

Maintainers should:

1. **Check alignment** with repository scope and style.
2. **Verify links and references** to existing documents.
3. **Ensure technical accuracy**, especially in compliance controls and procedures.
4. **Request revisions** when clarity or structure need improvement.
5. **Approve & Merge** when contributions meet requirements.

---

## File & Structure Management

Maintainers should oversee:

- Top‑level documents  
- The **[AI-BOM](./AI-BOM)** subdirectory and its internal structure  
- Evidence checklists and standardized formats  
- Document naming consistency  

---

## Branching & Release Strategy

- Use feature branches for major changes.
- Keep `main` stable and publication-ready.
- Ensure large structural updates are documented in PR descriptions.

---

## Best Practices for Maintainers

- Communicate decisions clearly.
- Provide rationale for rejections.
- Encourage open participation.
- Avoid adding proprietary or confidential material.
- Promote transparency in standards development.

---

## Contact & Escalation

- Use GitHub Issues for discussions.
- Use repository Discussions (if enabled) for broader topics.
- Security issues should follow the workflow in **[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)**.

---

Maintainers ensure this project remains a high-quality, open, and globally useful source for AI compliance and governance standards.
